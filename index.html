Our Legacy

A Heritage of Extraordinary Craft

Founded in 1979 in the ateliers of Paris, Namarata Mishra has spent nearly five decades perfecting the marriage of traditional craftsmanship with contemporary vision.

Every stitch tells a story. Every fabric is hand-selected from the world's finest mills — from the silk weavers of Como to the cashmere producers of Inner Mongolia. Our master artisans spend over 200 hours on each couture piece, ensuring every detail achieves perfection.

Today, Namarata Mishra stands as a beacon of luxury fashion — present in 35 countries, yet still crafting each piece with the same intimate attention that defined our very first collection.

Isabelle Élara, Creative Director
